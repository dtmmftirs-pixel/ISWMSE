    Material and Metallurgical Engineering - Indonesia

    Department of Materials and Metallurgical Engineering has the scientific field, expertise, and profession of Materials Engineering (Materials Science). In general, Materials and Metallurgical Engineering studies the properties of metal, ceramic, polymer, and composite materials along with their engineering technology. Materials engineering technology is related to material structures and manufacturing processes such as casting technology, welding, heat treatment, and others.

    Objective

    We are organizing this collaborative workshop in materials science and engineering to facilitate the exchange of scientific and technological knowledge, promote student education, and foster international partnerships among professors and students.

    Materials science and engineering have opened up new avenues for fundamental research, playing a crucial role in various technologies. Innovations in materials hold the potential to drive advancements in both science and technology. Each university and institution is actively researching materials science and engineering, specializing in their respective vital areas. Sharing these insights will deepen our understanding of materials, and this workshop provides a unique opportunity to initiate new joint research endeavors.

    While numerous international conferences are dedicated to materials science and engineering, it can be challenging for some students to participate in these events, and educational workshops for students are often limited. In our workshop, many students can present their research findings, and some may even become involved in the workshop's organization.

    Establishing international collaborations among professors and facilitating interactions among students is essential. Partnerships at the departmental level are critical for global engagement, and this joint workshop will enhance connections between departments and among individual professors and students.

    We anticipate lively discussions and interactions among participating students and professors. In the future, our goal is to sustain this platform for international collaboration and expand our network of collaborative partners to include other universities and institutions.
