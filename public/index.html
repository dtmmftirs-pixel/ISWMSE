Get To Know ISWMSE 2026

DTMM - ITS
Surabaya, Indonesia

Department of Materials and Metallurgical Engineering ITS focuses on the science and engineering of metals, ceramics, polymers, and composites. We pioneer technological innovation in casting, welding, heat treatment, advanced corrosion prevention, and novel functional materials for industrial advancement.

Visit Department Portal

Workshop Objectives

Materials science and engineering continue to unlock revolutionary solutions across clean energy, aerospace, medicine, and electronic hardware. While conventional conferences often present high barriers for early-career students, ISWMSE is deliberately engineered to bring students and professors together into collaborative, accessible scholarly dialogue.

Each participating university contributes its core scientific breakthroughs, igniting cross-border joint research initiatives and equipping students with international leadership competencies.
